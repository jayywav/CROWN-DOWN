Crown Down Rules

Goal

Finish with the lowest score. Cards are dealt face-up into four columns. Only each column's exposed top card is active.

Eliminations

If two or more exposed cards share a suit, a lower exposed card may be eliminated when a higher exposed card of that suit is showing. The eliminated card goes to Trash. Recheck the board after every elimination.

Movement

Normal cards never move between columns. Only an exposed Ace may move. An Ace can move to an empty column for free. Moving an Ace onto an occupied column uses 1 Paid Ace Move. You have 2 Paid Ace Moves per game.

Rounds

There is no timer. When you are done making moves, press Down / Deal Next 4. After all 52 cards are dealt, make your final moves and press Finish Normal Play.

Scoring

Each card remaining is +1. Each Ace that is not exposed on top adds another +1. If all four Aces are exposed, you earn Crown and subtract 2. Soft 4 is a Crown-adjusted score of 4 with more than four physical cards remaining. Perfect 4 means the only four cards remaining are the four Aces and scores exactly 4; Perfect 4 beats Soft 4.

Crown Drop / Ace Cut

If the opening four cards are all four Aces, Crown Drop is earned. After normal play ends, choose one of those original Aces. Every card stacked above that chosen Ace goes to Trash and the Ace remains. Ace Cut is the final action before scoring.
