Crown Down Rules

Eliminate

Only exposed top cards are active. Select a lower card, then a higher exposed card of the same suit to send the lower card to Trash. Eliminations are optional.

Move

Normal cards never move columns. Only exposed Aces move. Ace to an empty column is free. Ace to an occupied column costs one of your two Paid Ace Moves.

Rounds

No timer. Press Down / Deal Next 4 when finished. After all 52 cards are dealt, finish normal play and score.

Score

Each remaining card is +1. Each covered Ace adds +1. Four exposed Aces earns Crown −2. Exactly four remaining Aces is Perfect 4 and scores 4.

Crown Drop

If the opening four are all Aces, after normal play choose an original opening Ace. Everything above it goes to Trash, leaving that Ace. Then score.
