CROWN DOWN

How to Play

Goal

Finish with the lowest score. Only the top card of each of the four columns is active.

Eliminations

When exposed cards share a suit, a lower-ranked exposed card can be eliminated by a higher-ranked exposed card of that suit. The eliminated card goes to Trash. Recheck the newly exposed card immediately.

Movement

Normal cards never move between columns. Only an exposed Ace can move. Ace → empty column is free. Ace → occupied column costs 1 Paid Ace Move. You have 2 Paid Ace Moves per game.

Rounds

There is no timer. When you are finished making moves, press Down / Deal Next 4. Continue until all 52 cards have been dealt.

Scoring

Each card remaining is +1. Each Ace that is not exposed on top adds another +1. If all four Aces are exposed, you earn Crown and subtract 2. A Soft 4 is a Crown-adjusted score of 4 with more than four physical cards remaining. Perfect 4 means the only four cards remaining are the four Aces; it scores 4 and beats a Soft 4 tie.

Crown Drop

If the opening four cards are all four Aces, you earn Crown Drop. After normal play, choose one of those original Aces for an Ace Cut: every card above it goes to Trash and that Ace remains.
