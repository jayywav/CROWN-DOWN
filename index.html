Crown Down — Complete Rules

Four columns. Four Aces. Lowest wins.

1. Setup & Goal

Use one standard 52-card deck, no Jokers. Play in four face-up columns. Lowest final score wins.

VISUAL EXAMPLE

1 deck → 4 columns. Your job is to remove as many cards as possible and finish with the lowest score.

2. Deal & Rounds

Deal one card to each column to start. Each round, deal one new card onto every column. There are 13 rounds, using all 52 cards. Only the top card of each column is active. There is no timer. Make any moves you want, then press Down / Deal Next 4.

Every press deals one new card to each column. After 13 deals, the deck is empty.

3. Eliminating Cards

When exposed cards share a suit, a lower card can be removed if a higher card of that same suit is exposed. Ranks go 2 low through Ace high. Removed cards go to Trash. Eliminations are optional. After removing a card, the card underneath becomes active and can be used immediately.

4. Moving Cards

  • Any exposed card can move to an empty column for free.
  • Non-Aces cannot move onto an occupied column.
  • Only an exposed Ace can move onto an occupied column.
  • Ace → occupied column costs 1 Paid Ace Move.
  • You get 2 Paid Ace Moves for the whole game.
  • Moving a card exposes the card underneath it.

5. Ending Normal Play

After the 13th deal, make any final legal moves or eliminations. When finished, press Finish Normal Play. No normal moves can be made after that.

The last deal does not instantly end the game. Make your final legal plays first.

6. Crown Drop / Ace Cut

You earn Crown Drop only if the first four cards dealt are all four Aces. Four Aces appearing together later does not count. After normal play ends, choose one of those original opening Aces. Every card above that Ace in its current column goes to Trash, while the Ace stays. This is the final action before scoring.

7. Scoring

  • Each card left on the board = +1.
  • There is no extra penalty for a covered Ace.
  • If all four Aces remain on the board at the end, you normally earn All 4 Aces exposed → Crown bonus −2.
  • Near Miss: if exactly 5 cards remain and all four Aces are still on the board, you lose the Crown bonus and score 5.
  • Soft 4 (4S): 6 cards remain with all four Aces, so Ace bonus −2 makes the final score 4.
  • Perfect 4 (4P): exactly four cards remain and all four are Aces. The score is exactly 4; do not subtract Crown again.
  • If Perfect 4 and Soft 4 tie at 4, Perfect 4 wins.
  • HAND: the normal result.
  • No Crown bonus unless all 4 Aces are exposed on top at scoring. Covered/buried Aces do not count. If fewer than 4 Aces are exposed, your score is simply the number of cards left.
  • If all 4 Aces are exposed, subtract 2 from the card count, except for Near Miss and Perfect 4.
