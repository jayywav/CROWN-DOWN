How to Play

Only the top card of each column is active. If two exposed cards share a suit, the lower card can be eliminated by the higher card and goes to Trash.

Normal cards never move between columns. Only an exposed Ace may move. Ace → empty column is free. Ace → occupied column costs one of your 2 Paid Ace Moves.

Press Down / Deal Next 4 when you are finished with a round. After all 52 cards are dealt, finish normal play and score.

Each card left is +1. Each Ace not exposed on top is another +1. All four Aces exposed earns Crown (−2). Perfect 4 is exactly the four Aces remaining and scores 4.
